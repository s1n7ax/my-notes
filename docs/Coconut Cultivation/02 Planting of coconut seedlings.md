# Planting a coconut seedling

Source: [QR 03 - නිවැරදිව පොල් පැළයක් සිටවන ආකාරය](https://youtu.be/u85Qx7kpz6I) —
Coconut Research Institute Sri Lanka, 2022-03-29.

## 1. Choose the place

- In a home garden, pick a place that gets proper sunlight.
- A flat surface with sandy loam soil is ideal.
- Keep 26 feet between one plant and the next.

## 2. Dig the hole

- Sandy loam soil: 3 x 3 feet hole.
- While digging, keep the soil from the first 1 foot (topsoil) in one pile and the soil
  from below it (bottom soil) in a second pile.

## 3. Materials per hole

| Material | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Coconut husks | 2 layers |
| Organic fertilizer | 10 kg to 12 kg |
| Dolomite | 1 kg |
| YPM (young palm mixture) — wet zone | YPM-W, 1250 g |
| YPM (young palm mixture) — dry zone | YPM-D, 1 kg |

## 4. Fill the hole

1. Lay a layer of coconut husks at the bottom of the hole, with the cotton (soft, fibre)
   side facing up.
2. Add just enough topsoil to cover the husk layer.
3. Lay another layer of coconut husks the same way.
4. Add the rest of the topsoil on top of it.
5. Mix the organic fertilizer, YPM and dolomite into the bottom soil.
6. Fill the hole with that mix until 15 cm from the top are left unfilled. This gap helps
   water move towards the seedling.

## 5. Plant the seedling

1. Dig a small hole in the filled soil for the seedling.
2. Remove the bottom of the polybag and place the seedling in the hole.
3. Add some soil around it, and only then remove the polybag completely. This protects
   the roots of the seedling.

## 6. After planting

Add mulching around the seedling using coconut husks.
