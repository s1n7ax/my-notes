# Instructions for planting of coconut seedlings

Different official sources give different instructions. This note does not pick a
"correct" one. Each instruction is listed as the source states it, tagged with the
source reference. Newer sources may be more up to date, so the publish date is listed.

## Choosing the place

- **S2** — In a home garden, pick a place that gets proper sunlight.
- **S2** — A flat surface with sandy loam soil is ideal.

## Spacing

- **S2** — Keep 26 feet between one plant and the next.

## Hole size

- **S1** — Sandy soil: 3 x 3 feet hole is enough.
- **S1** — Hard gravelly soil: 4 x 4 feet hole is recommended.
- **S2** — Sandy loam soil: 3 x 3 feet hole.

## Handling the dug-out soil

- **S1** — While digging, keep the soil from the first 1.5 feet (topsoil) and the soil
  from the last 1.5 feet (bottom soil) in two separate piles beside the hole.
- **S2** — Keep the soil from the first 1 foot (topsoil) separate from the soil below it
  (bottom soil).

## Materials per hole

- **S1** — Organic fertilizer: 10 kg
- **S1** — Dolomite: 1 kg
- **S1** — YPM (young palm mixture):
  - Wet zone and intermediate zone: 1.25 kg
  - Dry zone: 1 kg
- **S1** — Coconut husks: 30/35
- **S2** — Organic fertilizer: 10 kg to 12 kg
- **S2** — Dolomite: 1 kg
- **S2** — YPM (young palm mixture):
  - Wet zone: YPM-W, 1250 g
  - Dry zone: YPM-D, 1 kg

## Filling the hole

- **S1** — Steps in order:
  1. Lay a layer of coconut husks at the bottom of the hole, with the cotton (soft, fibre)
     side facing up.
  2. Add a thin layer of the bottom soil.
  3. Lay another layer of coconut husks the same way.
  4. Add another thin layer of the bottom soil.
  5. Mix the topsoil with YPM and the organic fertilizer, and fill the hole with it until
     6 inches from the top are left unfilled.

- **S2** — Steps in order:
  1. Lay a layer of coconut husks at the bottom of the hole, with the cotton (soft, fibre)
     side facing up.
  2. Add just enough topsoil to cover the husk layer.
  3. Lay another layer of coconut husks the same way.
  4. Add the rest of the topsoil on top of it.
  5. Mix the organic fertilizer, YPM and dolomite together with the bottom soil.
  6. Fill the hole with that mix until 15 cm from the top are left unfilled. This gap helps
     water move towards the seedling.

## Planting the seedling

- **S1** — Dig a small hole in the filled soil and place the coconut seedling in it.
- **S1** — Mix dolomite into the soil around the seedling.
- **S2** — Steps in order:
  1. Dig a small hole in the filled soil for the seedling.
  2. Remove the bottom of the polybag and place the seedling in the hole.
  3. Add some soil around it, and only then remove the polybag completely. This protects
     the roots of the seedling.

## After planting

- **S2** — Add mulching around the seedling using coconut husks.
