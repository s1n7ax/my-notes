# Instructions for planting of coconut seedlings

Different official sources give different instructions. This note does not pick a
"correct" one. Each instruction is listed as the source states it, tagged with the
source reference. Newer sources may be more up to date, so the publish date is listed.

## Hole size

- **S1** — Sandy soil: 3 x 3 feet hole is enough.
- **S1** — Hard gravelly soil: 4 x 4 feet hole is recommended.

## Handling the dug-out soil

- **S1** — While digging, keep the soil from the first 1.5 feet (topsoil) and the soil
  from the last 1.5 feet (bottom soil) in two separate piles beside the hole.

## Materials per hole

- **S1** — Organic fertilizer: 10 kg
- **S1** — Dolomite: 1 kg
- **S1** — YPM (young palm mixture):
  - Wet zone and intermediate zone: 1.25 kg
  - Dry zone: 1 kg
- **S1** — Coconut husks: 30/35

## Filling the hole

- **S1** — Steps in order:
  1. Lay a layer of coconut husks at the bottom of the hole, with the cotton (soft, fibre)
     side facing up.
  2. Add a thin layer of the bottom soil.
  3. Lay another layer of coconut husks the same way.
  4. Add another thin layer of the bottom soil.
  5. Mix the topsoil with YPM and the organic fertilizer, and fill the hole with it until
     6 inches from the top are left unfilled.

## Planting the seedling

- **S1** — Dig a small hole in the filled soil and place the coconut seedling in it.
- **S1** — Mix dolomite into the soil around the seedling.
